Knowledge Base

Static reference content — there's no Knowledge Base API endpoint yet, so this isn't ticket data.

My WiFi keeps disconnecting

Try forgetting and re-joining the network first. If it keeps happening, log a ticket under the Network category with your building and room so a technician can check for interference.

How ticket priority works

High priority targets a 4 hour response window, Medium 24 hours, and Low 72 hours, based on when the ticket was created.

Attaching a photo to a ticket

Use the Attachment field when creating a ticket — JPEG, PNG, WEBP or GIF, up to 5MB.

Rating a resolved ticket

Once a ticket is marked Resolved or Closed, open it from My Tickets to leave a star rating and comment.
